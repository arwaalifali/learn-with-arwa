About Arwa

A personal mission to make Islamic learning warm and accessible.

My Story

As-salamu alaykum. My name is Arwa and I am 11 years old. I built this website out of my deep love for Allah, the Holy Quran and the Prophets peace be upon them all. I wanted to create a peaceful and beautiful online space where Muslims and learners everywhere can read, reflect and reconnect with the guidance of Islam at their own pace. I hope this website brings you closer to Allah and fills your heart with peace and knowledge. JazakAllahu Khayran for visiting Learn with Arwa.

Mission

To provide clear, beautiful and authentic Islamic content that helps individuals and families strengthen their relationship with Allah through Quran, Hadith and Duas.

Vision

To grow Learn with Arwa into a trusted home for seekers of knowledge where faith, tradition and good character are nurtured with consistency and mercy.
