Morning Duas

أَصْبَحْنَا وَأَصْبَحَ الْمُلْكُ لِلَّهِ

We have reached the morning and at this very time unto Allah belongs all sovereignty

Muslim

اللَّهُمَّ بِكَ أَصْبَحْنَا وَبِكَ أَمْسَيْنَا

O Allah by You we enter the morning and by You we enter the evening

Abu Dawud

أَصْبَحْنَا عَلَى فِطْرَةِ الْإِسْلَامِ

We have entered the morning upon the natural religion of Islam

Ahmad

اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ رَبِّي لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ

O Allah You are my Lord there is no god but You

Bukhari

سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ

Glory be to Allah and praise be to Him

Bukhari

Evening Duas

أَمْسَيْنَا وَأَمْسَى الْمُلْكُ لِلَّهِ

We have reached the evening and at this very time unto Allah belongs all sovereignty

Muslim

اللَّهُمَّ بِكَ أَمْسَيْنَا وَبِكَ أَصْبَحْنَا

O Allah by You we enter the evening and by You we enter the morning

Abu Dawud

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَمْسَيْتُ أُشْهِدُكَ

O Allah I have reached the evening and call You to witness

Abu Dawud

Before Eating

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ

In the name of Allah

Abu Dawud

اللَّهُمَّ بَارِكْ لَنَا فِيمَا رَزَقْتَنَا

O Allah bless us in what You have provided for us

Ibn Al-Sunni

After Eating

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَطْعَمَنَا

All praise is for Allah who gave us food and drink

Abu Dawud

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ حَمْدًا كَثِيرًا طَيِّبًا

All praise be to Allah much good and blessed praise

Bukhari

Before Sleeping

بِاسْمِكَ اللَّهُمَّ أَمُوتُ وَأَحْيَا

In Your name O Allah I die and I live

Bukhari

اللَّهُمَّ قِنِي عَذَابَكَ يَوْمَ تَبْعَثُ عِبَادَكَ

O Allah protect me from Your punishment on the day You resurrect Your servants

Abu Dawud

اللَّهُمَّ بِاسْمِكَ أَحْيَا وَأَمُوتُ

O Allah in Your name I live and die

Bukhari

After Waking Up

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَحْيَانَا بَعْدَ مَا أَمَاتَنَا

All praise is for Allah who gave us life after having taken it from us

Bukhari

أَصْبَحْتُ وَأَصْبَحَ الْمُلْكُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ

I have entered the morning and the dominion belongs to Allah Lord of the worlds

Muslim

Entering Home

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ خَيْرَ الْمَوْلِجِ

O Allah I ask You for the good of the entering and the good of the leaving

Abu Dawud

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ وَلَجْنَا وَبِسْمِ اللَّهِ خَرَجْنَا

In the name of Allah we enter and in the name of Allah we leave

Abu Dawud

Leaving Home

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ تَوَكَّلْتُ عَلَى اللَّهِ

In the name of Allah I place my trust in Allah

Abu Dawud

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ أَنْ أَضِلَّ أَوْ أُضَلَّ

O Allah I seek refuge in You from going astray or being led astray

Abu Dawud

Entering Masjid

اللَّهُمَّ افْتَحْ لِي أَبْوَابَ رَحْمَتِكَ

O Allah open the doors of Your mercy for me

Muslim

أَعُوذُ بِاللَّهِ الْعَظِيمِ وَبِوَجْهِهِ الْكَرِيمِ

I seek refuge in Allah the Almighty and in His noble face

Abu Dawud

Leaving Masjid

اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ مِنْ فَضْلِكَ

O Allah I ask You of Your bounty

Muslim

Travelling

اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ

Allah is the Greatest Allah is the Greatest Allah is the Greatest

Muslim

سُبْحَانَ الَّذِي سَخَّرَ لَنَا هَذَا

Glory be to Him who has subjected this to us

Abu Dawud

اللَّهُمَّ إِنَّا نَسْأَلُكَ فِي سَفَرِنَا هَذَا الْبِرَّ

O Allah we ask You on this journey for goodness and piety

Muslim

Forgiveness

أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ الْعَظِيمَ الَّذِي لَا إِلَهَ إِلَّا هُوَ

I seek forgiveness from Allah the Almighty besides whom there is no god

Abu Dawud

رَبِّ اغْفِرْ لِي وَتُبْ عَلَيَّ

My Lord forgive me and accept my repentance

Bukhari

اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِي ذَنْبِي كُلَّهُ

O Allah forgive all my sins

Muslim

Anxiety and Sadness

O Allah I am Your servant son of Your servant

Ahmad

There is no god but Allah the Almighty the Forbearing

Bukhari

حَسْبُنَا اللَّهُ وَنِعْمَ الْوَكِيلُ

Quran 17:24

For Protection

I seek refuge in the perfect words of Allah from every devil and every poisonous pest

Bukhari

In the name of Allah with whose name nothing can cause harm

Abu Dawud
